Composición introspectiva sobre la resiliencia somática y la gratitud biológica.

### Notas del Autor y Fundamento Somático
> *«Frente a las metáforas abstractas del amor, el corazón es primariamente una bomba muscular que late cien mil veces al día sin pedir permiso ni requerir aprobación mental. Esta canción es un homenaje a esa fidelidad biológica que nos sostiene vivos aun cuando el ánimo decae.»*

---

### Letra Completa

**(Verse 1)**  
Walked through the ashes of yesterday’s fire,  
Carrying stones that were never mine.  
Searching the clouds for a distant sign,  
While beneath the ribs, a fire burns fine.  

**(Pre-Chorus)**  
It doesn't ask for a map or a crown,  
It doesn't sleep when the night comes down.  
Faithful and steady, quiet and deep,  
A sacred promise it chose to keep.  

**(Chorus)**  
This is the medicine, this is the cure,  
Not found in potions, but raw and pure.  
Through every fracture and every song,  
Thank you Heart, for beating strong.  

**(Verse 2)**  
I sought for answers in books of stone,  
Forgot the temple made of bone.  
Each cell a sister, each breath a wave,  
Nothing to conquer, nothing to save.  

**(Chorus)**  
This is the medicine, this is the cure,  
Not found in potions, but raw and pure.  
Through every fracture and every song,  
Thank you Heart, for beating strong.  

**(Outro)**  
Beating strong... in the deep dark night.  
Beating strong... welcoming the light.  
Pulse of the earth, where I belong:  
Thank you Heart, for beating strong.
